Help for Tic Tac Whoa!, a game by Zquid, LLC.

Contact us

Email support@zquidgames.com. Tell us what phone you have and what happened, and we will get back to you as soon as we can. Please do not send us passwords, payment details or other private information.

How do I play?

The big board has nine mini-boards. Win three mini-boards in a row to win the game. The square you pick inside a mini-board decides which mini-board your opponent plays in next. In the app, tap How to play on the home screen for a short guide.

What are Ability Cards?

In Whoa Mode you bring a few Ability Cards, such as Eraser or Teleport, that can change the board. Each can be used once per match. You earn new cards by leveling up, and cards are never sold.

Playing a friend online

Choose Friend Online, then Make a game to get a four-letter code. Your friend chooses Join a game and types the code. If the app closes in the middle of a match, reopen it within a minute and you will rejoin the match.

Whoa! Coins and purchases

You earn Whoa! Coins by playing. Coins can be spent on looks, music and arenas that never change how the game plays. If you buy coins with real money, the purchase goes through the App Store and the usual Apple rules apply, including Apple's own controls for children's accounts. To ask about a refund, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

My progress or coins look wrong

Your progress is stored on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it. Email us if something looks wrong and tell us what you saw.

Report a problem or inappropriate content

Players cannot type their own names or chat in the game. If you see a name or anything else that seems wrong, email us with a screenshot.
