Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Whoa! by Zquid, LLC. Last updated: November 20, 2026.

Draft: this policy is being reviewed by our attorney and may change before the game launches.

Zquid, LLC ("we", "us") makes Tic Tac Whoa!. The game is meant for players aged 9 and up, and we designed it to collect as little information as possible. This page explains what the game does and does not collect.

The short version

  • No passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, photos or contacts.
  • To play friends online, the game makes an anonymous account: a random code, not your name.
  • No chat and no free-typed names. Players pick names from lists, so nobody can type their own words into the game.
  • A few light ads between matches, never during play. Ads are never personalized, and we never track you across other apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics tools.
  • We do not sell personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your level, experience points, unlocked cards, Whoa! Coins, items you bought, settings and the name the game gave you are stored on your phone. If you delete the app, they are deleted.

Your online account

The first time you use Friends or play online, the game asks our server for an anonymous account: a random account code and a secret key, kept safely on your phone. We never ask for a name, email address or password. Our server stores:

  • the account code, and the secret key in a scrambled form that cannot be read back;
  • the name the game gave you (picked from lists) and the look of your marks;
  • your friends list, and whether you are online right now, which only your friends can see;
  • a hidden skill rating, used to find fair opponents;
  • your weekly tournament entries, points and results.

You can delete your online account at any time in the game's Settings. This removes it from our server, along with your friends list, rating and tournament history.

What is sent during an online match

During a match the server receives only what it needs to run it: the match code, the moves made, the name shown to the other player and the look of your marks. We do not keep the moves after the match, apart from the result used for your skill rating and tournament points. Like most online services, the server keeps short-lived technical logs that help us keep the service running and secure. Those logs may include your device's network (IP) address and are deleted after a short time.

Ads

Tic Tac Whoa! shows a small number of ads between matches. Ads never appear during a match, and new players see none until they have learned the game. Some ads are optional: you can choose to watch one in return for a few Whoa! Coins.

We use one advertising partner, set up for apps that children may use:

  • Ads are chosen by what is on screen (contextual), never by your interests or past activity.
  • The game does not use your phone's advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages.

To show an ad, our partner receives basic technical information: your device model, its system version and language, your approximate location (country or region, worked out from the network address), and whether an ad was shown or tapped. We will name our advertising partner here before ads appear in the game.

Purchases

If the game offers purchases, such as Whoa! Coins or removing ads, they are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details. Apple tells us that a purchase was completed so we can add what you bought.

Children

The game is designed so that children can play without giving us personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described on this page, and we do not use it for personalized advertising. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Delete your online account in the game's Settings.
  • If the game offers a purchase to remove ads, it turns off the ads between matches. Optional ads you choose to watch stay available.
  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on your phone.
  • Contact us with any question about your information, using the address below.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date above.
