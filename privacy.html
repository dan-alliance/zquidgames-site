Privacy Policy

Tic Tac Whoa! by Zquid, LLC. Last updated: November 14, 2026.

Draft: this policy is being reviewed by our attorney and may change before the game launches.

Zquid, LLC ("we", "us") makes Tic Tac Whoa!. The game is meant for players aged 9 and up, and we designed it to collect as little information as possible. This page explains what the game does and does not collect.

The short version

  • No accounts, passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, locations, photos or contacts.
  • No chat and no free-typed names. Players pick names from lists, so nobody can type their own words into the game.
  • No ads, and no third-party advertising or analytics tools.
  • We do not sell personal information.

What stays on your phone

Your level, experience points, unlocked cards, Whoa! Coins, items you bought, settings and the name the game gave you are stored on your phone. We do not receive them. If you delete the app, they are deleted.

What is sent when you play online

When you play a friend online, the game connects to our game server. The server receives only what it needs to run the match: the match code, the moves made, the name shown to your friend (picked from lists), and the look of your marks. We do not keep this information after the match, apart from short-lived technical logs that help us keep the service running and secure. Those logs may include your device's network (IP) address and are deleted after a short time.

Purchases

If the game offers purchases of Whoa! Coins, they are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details. Apple tells us that a purchase was completed so we can add the coins.

Children

The game is designed so that children can play without giving us personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can delete the app at any time to remove everything stored on your phone. To ask about information related to online matches, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the date above.
